## Full clause — "Eligibility; Licensure Representation and Warranty"

**1. Eligibility.** Membership in AXESS (the "Network," operated by [Citicore Properties Inc / legal entity]) is limited to individuals who hold a current, active real estate salesperson or broker license issued by the [Massachusetts Board of Registration of Real Estate Brokers and Salespersons] (or, as the Network expands, the equivalent regulator of another state in which the Network operates) and who are in good standing with that regulator and with their sponsoring broker/brokerage. Membership is offered to individual licensed agents. A brokerage does not need to enroll or approve an agent's membership.

**2. Representations and Warranties.** By creating an account and by accepting these terms, you represent and warrant that: (a) you are a currently licensed real estate agent in good standing; (b) your legal name, license number, sponsoring brokerage, contact information, and all other information you provide are true, accurate, and complete; (c) you are not misrepresenting your identity, your licensure, or your authority to act; (d) you will only post, share, or solicit deals and client needs that you are authorized to share; and (e) your use of the Network will comply with all applicable laws and regulations, your license and brokerage obligations, fair-housing requirements, applicable MLS rules, and the National Association of REALTORS® Clear Cooperation Policy, including any obligation to timely submit a listing to the MLS and to obtain any client or seller consent required to market a property off-market or pre-market.

**3. Ongoing Duty; Authorization to Verify.** Your representations in Section 2 are continuing and must remain true for as long as you are a member. You agree to promptly update your account and to notify AXESS if any of them ceases to be true — including if your license lapses, is suspended, revoked, or placed on inactive status, or if you change or leave your sponsoring brokerage. You authorize AXESS to verify your license and standing at any time, including at signup and on a periodic basis, through public license-lookup resources maintained by the applicable regulator and through any other lawful means, and you consent to AXESS relying on those resources.

**4. Verification; Pending Status.** New members are admitted in a pending status and may browse the Network and complete a profile, but cannot post deals or client needs, request introductions, or access other members' contact details until AXESS has verified their license and standing and approved the account. Verification is a convenience and a screening measure; it does not make AXESS a guarantor of any member's licensure, identity, conduct, or the accuracy of any information or deal posted on the Network. Members remain responsible for independently verifying the identity, licensure, authority, and information of anyone they transact with.

**5. Termination Without Refund for Misrepresentation.** Any false, misleading, or materially incomplete statement regarding your identity, licensure, standing, brokerage, or authority — whether made at signup or at any time during membership — is a material breach and is grounds for immediate suspension or termination of your membership by AXESS, in its sole discretion and without notice, and **without refund** of any fees paid. AXESS may also decline or revoke any badge (e.g., "Verified," "Producer," "Proof of Funds") at its discretion.

**6. Indemnification.** You agree to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless AXESS, [Citicore Properties Inc], and its officers, employees, and agents from and against any claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or related to: (a) any breach of your representations, warranties, or obligations under this Agreement; (b) any deal, listing, client need, message, document, or other content you post or share on the Network; (c) your violation of any law, regulation, license or brokerage obligation, fair-housing requirement, MLS rule, or the Clear Cooperation Policy; or (d) any transaction or dealing between you and another member or third party.

**7. Personal, Non-Transferable Membership.** Your membership and account are personal to you as an individual licensed agent. You may not share, transfer, assign, resell, or provide access to your account or its contents to any other person, including other agents at your brokerage, without AXESS's prior written consent. Login links are for your use only. Team, brokerage-roster, or consolidated-billing arrangements, if offered, are separate and must be arranged with AXESS.

**8. No Legal Advice; Member Responsibility.** AXESS is a private, invite-only networking platform for licensed agents. It is not a real estate broker, an MLS, or a listing service, and it does not market properties to the public. Deal details, figures, and any built-in analysis are member-submitted and/or estimates and are not verified or endorsed by AXESS. Nothing provided by AXESS is legal, tax, or brokerage-compliance advice, and members are solely responsible for their own compliance.
