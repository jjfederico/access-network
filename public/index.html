FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my property address really private?
Yes. The address is stored on our servers and never sent to a buyer's browser until you approve that specific introduction. Everyone else sees the submarket, the numbers, and your description — never the address.
Who can join?
Licensed Massachusetts real estate agents. To join, you certify your active license and brokerage — and membership can be revoked at any time if that's not accurate. That's what keeps the network high-signal and spam-free.
What does it cost, and are there deal fees?
The first 100 members join completely free. Once we reach 100, those founding members lock in a $25/month rate for life, and everyone who joins after that pays $50/month. We'll always tell you before anything is charged. Posting deals is free and unlimited, and commissions are set by the seller on each deal — we never take a cut of your commission.
Is this an MLS or an open marketplace?
Neither. AXESS is a private, invite-only, agent-to-agent network for off-market opportunities — the exact opposite of a public, open-market platform. Deals move directly between licensed agents and never appear publicly unless you choose to take them public elsewhere.
How fast can I list a deal?
About 60 seconds. Add the address (kept private), a submarket, price, commission, and a short description — you're live.
