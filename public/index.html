FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my property address really private?
Yes. The address is stored on our servers and never sent to a buyer's browser until you approve that specific introduction. Everyone else sees the submarket, the numbers, and your description — never the address.
Who can join?
Licensed Massachusetts real estate agents. Every member is reviewed and approved by hand — we verify your license and brokerage before you're in. That's what keeps the network high-signal and spam-free.
What does it cost, and are there deal fees?
Right now it's free — founding members join at no cost while we build out the network. Membership will be $50/month afterward, and we'll always tell you before anything is charged. Posting deals is free and unlimited, and commissions are set by the seller on each deal — we never take a cut of your commission.
Is this an MLS or an open marketplace?
Neither. ACCESS is a private, invite-only, agent-to-agent network for off-market opportunities — the exact opposite of a public, open-market platform. Deals move directly between vetted agents and never appear publicly unless you choose to take them public elsewhere.
How fast can I list a deal?
About 60 seconds. Add the address (kept private), a submarket, price, commission, and a short description — you're live.
